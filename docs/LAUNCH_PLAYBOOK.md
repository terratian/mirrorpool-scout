# Mirror Pool Scout: zero-budget launch playbook

## 0. Positioning (read this first)
Shipping-aware eBay grading is the HOOK (easy to show in 5 seconds), not the product. The product is a buying workstation:
"Your MTG shopping list that finds the real deals, so you don't open 8 tabs."
Pillars: (1) one Buy List from any deck site/app, no retyping, minus what you own; (2) 8 sources one click away, cheaper-printing finder; (3) delivered-price deal grading + foil flash on real deals; (4) arbitrage: paper vs MTGO tix, flip calculator with fees; (5) price-drop alerts; (6) one portable backup file.
Audience split: players building decks cheaply (pillars 1, 2, 5), flippers (3, 4). Make separate clips for each; do not pitch both in one post.

Clip plan (one feature per clip): A "Paste a deck, see the real cost" (pillars 1-2). B "Shipping makes cheap cards a trap" (3). C "What I keep after fees" (4). D "Get pinged when it drops" (5).

## 1. Get it into free stores (no fees)
**Microsoft Edge Add-ons** (free): partner.microsoft.com/dashboard/microsoftedge, register with a Microsoft account, upload `mirrorpool-scout-v1.5.0.zip` (the Chrome zip works as-is), fill in listing, submit. Review usually takes days.
**Firefox (addons.mozilla.org)** (free): addons.mozilla.org/developers, upload `mirrorpool-scout-firefox-v1.5.0.zip`. Choose "On this site" for the listed version. Mozilla asks for source if code is minified; ours is plain, so say so. UNTESTED in Firefox from the sandbox: load it via about:debugging first and click through Buy List, Collection, Settings.
**Until approved:** GitHub Releases. Attach both zips to a release on terratian/mirrorpool-scout and link it everywhere.
**Chrome Web Store** later, once the $5 is no object.

Listing text (reuse everywhere):
- Title: Mirror Pool Scout: MTG Buy List, Deals and Flip Calculator
- Short: Track your MTG Buy List, grade eBay listings by delivered price (shipping included), calculate flip profit after fees, get price-drop alerts.
- Privacy line: Runs in your browser. Your lists never leave your device. Card prices come from Scryfall. Some eBay/TCGplayer links are affiliate links.

## 2. The demo clip (make this once, reuse as GIF + vertical video)
Screen-record 30-45 seconds, no voiceover needed, add captions:
1. (0-5s) Paste a decklist (or click "Send this deck to Scout" on a deck site). Caption: "Any deck list, no retyping."
2. (5-12s) Buy List fills with prices. Caption: "Market price per card, and what you still need."
3. (12-25s) Click the eBay link. Results page: green badges "$1.35 + $0.78 shipping = $2.13 delivered". Caption: "eBay's price filter ignores shipping. This doesn't."
4. (25-35s) A deal row shimmers with the foil pill. Caption: "Real deals light up."
5. (35-45s) Flip calculator: net profit after eBay fees. Caption: "What you actually keep."
End card: free to use, $9 once to remove the watermark, link.

## 3. Posts (be honest, say you built it; follow each community's self-promo rules, read the sidebar first)
**r/mtgfinance / Discords (text post):**
Title: "I built a free browser extension that grades eBay MTG listings by delivered price, since eBay's price filter ignores shipping"
Body: the problem in 2 sentences (a $1.35 card with $0.78 shipping is not a deal), what it does in 4 bullets, the GIF, and: "I'm a disabled player building this solo. It's free with a watermark, $9 once removes it. Looking for feedback, especially cards or sites it gets wrong." Ask a question at the end. Reply to every comment.
**Facebook MTG groups:** short version, GIF first, one sentence of story, link in a comment if the group bans links in posts.
**TikTok / Reels / Shorts (vertical):** hook in first 2 seconds as text: "Stop overpaying shipping on cheap MTG cards." Then the clip. Caption: story in one line + "link in bio". Post 3 clips over a week: (a) shipping trick, (b) paste a deck and see the cost, (c) flip calculator on a real card you sold. Real purchases beat staged ones.
**Your eBay orders:** insert card: "I built the tool I use to find these. Free: [link]".

## 4. Measure (cheap)
Add the tracked link to the banner later. Count: installs (store dashboards), Gumroad sales, comments asking for features. First 20 users: ask what they'd pay for.
