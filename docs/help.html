Mirror Pool Scout: Help and Tutorial

A short guide. Each section is one thing you can do. Questions or bugs: open an issue or email joshuasultan@gmail.com.

1. Make a Buy List from a deck

  1. Open the Scout dashboard (click the extension icon) and go to Buy List.
  2. Paste a deck list in the box and click Save List & Get Prices. It reads lists from MTGGoldfish, MTGDecks, Moxfield, Archidekt, Arena, MTGO, TCGplayer, ManaBox and CSV files.
  3. Or open a deck on MTGGoldfish, Moxfield, Archidekt, TappedOut, Deckstats, MTGTop8, EDHREC or AetherHub and click Import from Open Deck Page (or the "Send this deck to Scout" button on the page).
If a card shows no price, it may be spelled differently. Scout tries a fuzzy match and tells you which names it could not find.

2. Only buy what you still need

Go to My Collection, import your ManaBox, Moxfield, Deckbox or TCGplayer collection file, then tick Subtract cards I already own on the Buy List.

3. Find real deals (shipping included)

Click the eBay BIN or free-ship link on a card. The eBay results page then shows a badge on each listing with price + shipping = delivered total, graded against your target (85% of market by default). Good deals glow, and the Buy List row lights up with a pill. Set your usual shipping cost and cards-per-order in the shipping box above the table.

4. Compare stores: landed cost

The Landed cost by store card shows the estimated total at each store (price + shipping + tax) and the cheapest single store or split order. These are estimates. Open Edit shipping, tax and price assumptions and set each store to what you really pay.

5. Flip calculator

On any card, click More ▾ → Flip calculator. Enter buy price, sell price and shipping. It subtracts eBay or TCGplayer fees and shows your profit, ROI, break-even and the most you can pay for your target return. Fee presets are starting points; check the current fees on eBay and TCGplayer.

6. Price alerts

In the Price alerts card, set a target for a card (or use the 85% / 70% of market buttons), tick Alerts on, and choose how often to check. Chrome must be open for alerts to run.

7. Back up your decks and bookmarks

Go to Settings → Backup & restore and click Download backup. Keep the file somewhere safe. After an update Scout also keeps an automatic copy you can restore from the same card. Bookmarks can be exported to your browser or a spreadsheet.

Moving between installs: your data lives in the browser. If you switch from a sideloaded copy to the store version (or to another computer), download a backup first and restore it in the new one.

8. The $9 Lifetime Key

Scout is free to use with a watermark. The key removes it. After buying on Gumroad, copy your key from the receipt email, open Settings and paste it in. Trouble? Email me with your receipt.

Privacy and affiliate links

Your lists stay in your browser. Some eBay and TCGplayer links are affiliate links, which cost you nothing. See the privacy policy.
